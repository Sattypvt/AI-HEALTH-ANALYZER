About This Project

An AI-powered health risk prediction system built as a college demo project.

🎯 Project Goal

This application uses Machine Learning to predict a user's risk level for three major lifestyle diseases based on clinical and lifestyle inputs.

🔬 Diseases Predicted

❤️ Heart Disease

Cardiovascular conditions caused by high BP, cholesterol, and smoking habits.

🩸 Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes driven by high glucose levels, obesity, and sedentary lifestyle.

💉 Hypertension

High blood pressure linked to stress, BMI, cholesterol, and salt intake.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. User enters health metrics and selects symptoms.
  2. Inputs are scaled using StandardScaler.
  3. Three separate Random Forest classifiers predict each disease.
  4. Symptom data adjusts probabilities for more realistic results.
  5. A Health Score (0–100) is computed from all metrics.
  6. Results and history are saved for trend tracking.
